## Part A — exploration

**Budget: about 450 words, 600 hard ceiling.** Push past the theme's first obvious reading, but do it briskly. No preamble, no restating the theme back at me.

- **Angles:** exactly four sticker concepts, **one or two sentences each**. Include at least one built on an object-with-a-face rather than the obvious mascot, and at least one carrying a small joke. Then name your pick in a single sentence saying why it beats the other three — no comparison table.
- **The chosen design:** one short paragraph fixing the subject, pose, expression, and the one or two supporting details that earn their place. Give it a personality in a few words, not a description of a shape.
- **Contour:** one or two lines describing the outer die-cut shape as a simple closed curve, and naming anything on the subject that has to be tucked in, thickened, or absorbed so the cut works.
- **Palette:** one line — dominant, supports, accent, with named colours, plus a note on how it holds up against both a white and a dark surface.
- **Readability fixes:** a short bullet list only — every element to enlarge, separate, simplify, or delete so it holds at thumbnail size. Just the fixes, not the reasoning.

Skip anything not on that list: no tradeoff essays, no runner-up post-mortems, no notes on your own process. Mention finishes like holographic or transparent vinyl only if they change the artwork itself.

## Part B — the final prompt

End your response with the final image-generation prompt for the chosen design, and nothing after it.

Format it as a single fenced code block labelled `FINAL IMAGE PROMPT`, so it can be copied cleanly. Use the labelled-block template from step 1 in order: Format → Concept → Subject → Details → Line & fill → Palette → Contour → Layout → Constraints last.

**Length: 180–250 words.** Long enough to carry the full spec, short enough that no single instruction gets diluted. If you are over, cut adjectives before you cut any contour or layout rule.

Hard requirements for that prompt:

- Its **first sentence** states this is a **die-cut vinyl sticker design in flat graphic vector style**. Opening words set the mode for everything after.
- It specifies **one single subject**, centred, on a **plain flat background**, with even margin and nothing touching the image edge.
- It calls for a **simple closed die-cut contour with a white keyline border**, and states the border weight relative to the subject.
- It names the flat colours explicitly rather than saying "colourful".
- Any literal text appears in quotes with exact casing, kept very short.
- The exclusion list comes **last**, after every positive descriptor, and includes no background scene, no sticker sheet or multiple stickers, no drop shadow, and no watermark.
- It is self-contained: someone pasting it cold, with no memory of this conversation, gets the same image. No "as described above", no "the design from earlier".
- One prompt only. No variants, no alternates, no commentary after the code block.
