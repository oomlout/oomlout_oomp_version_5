## Part A — exploration

**Budget: about 450 words, 600 hard ceiling.** Push past the theme's first obvious reading, but do it briskly. No preamble, no restating the theme back at me.

- **Angles:** exactly four pattern directions, **one or two sentences each**. Include at least one that goes beyond confetti-and-balloons to something theme-native, and at least one built on an unexpected repeat structure. Then name your pick in a single sentence saying why it beats the other three — no comparison table.
- **The motif set:** a short bullet list naming every motif by tier — 2–3 heroes, 3–4 secondaries, 2–3 fillers — with a few words on each. This is the spine of the pattern, so be specific rather than saying "party icons".
- **Layout:** one or two lines fixing the repeat logic, target density, spacing, and how much rotation variation to use.
- **Palette:** one line — background field colour plus named motif colours, with a note on value separation.
- **Repeat fixes:** a short bullet list only — anything to enlarge, thin out, rotate differently, or delete to break up accidental lanes, clusters, or empty patches. Just the fixes, not the reasoning.

Skip anything not on that list: no tradeoff essays, no runner-up post-mortems, no notes on your own process.

## Part B — the final prompt

End your response with the final image-generation prompt for the chosen pattern, and nothing after it.

Format it as a single fenced code block labelled `FINAL IMAGE PROMPT`, so it can be copied cleanly. Use the labelled-block template from step 1 in order: Format → Concept → Hero motifs → Secondary → Filler → Layout → Palette → Edges → Constraints last.

**Length: 180–250 words.** Long enough to carry the full spec, short enough that no single instruction gets diluted. If you are over, cut adjectives before you cut any layout or edge rule.

Hard requirements for that prompt:

- Its **first sentence** states this is a **square all-over repeating pattern in flat vector style**. Opening words set the mode for everything after.
- It names the motifs by tier with their relative sizes, rather than listing them as one undifferentiated pile.
- It states that the pattern **fills the entire square edge to edge**, with **motifs running off all four sides** and consistent density in the corners.
- It gives the background field colour and the named motif colours explicitly.
- The exclusion list comes **last**, after every positive descriptor, and leads with no border, no frame, no margin, no blank corners, no single centred focal motif.
- It is self-contained: someone pasting it cold, with no memory of this conversation, gets the same image. No "as described above", no "the motifs from earlier".
- One prompt only. No variants, no alternates, no commentary after the code block.
