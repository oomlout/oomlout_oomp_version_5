## Part A — exploration

**Budget: about 450 words, 600 hard ceiling.** Push past the theme's first obvious reading, but do it briskly. No preamble, no restating the theme back at me.

- **Angles:** exactly three directions for the set, **one or two sentences each** — for example mascot-led, badge-led, word-and-number-led. Then name your pick in a single sentence saying why it beats the other two.
- **The piece list:** a numbered list of every cutout in the set, one short line each, covering the mix of categories. Stay inside your total count. Make them theme-native rather than default party shapes.
- **Text:** every piece of lettering as an exact quoted string with exact casing, plus the backing shape that encloses it. If a piece has no text, say so.
- **Cohesion:** one or two lines fixing shared outline weight, corner radius, face style, and the named palette.
- **Cut fixes:** a short bullet list only — every neck to thicken, appendage to merge, island to absorb, inside corner to round, and base to solidify. Just the fixes, not the reasoning.

Skip anything not on that list: no tradeoff essays, no runner-up post-mortems, no notes on your own process.

## Part B — the final prompt

End your response with the final image-generation prompt for the chosen set, and nothing after it.

Format it as a single fenced code block labelled `FINAL IMAGE PROMPT`, so it can be copied cleanly. Use the labelled-block template from step 1 in order: Format → Concept → Pieces → Layout → Cut spec → Line & fill → Palette → Background → Constraints last.

**Length: 180–250 words.** Long enough to carry the full spec, short enough that no single instruction gets diluted. If you are over, cut pieces before you cut any cut-safety rule — fewer, larger, sturdier pieces beat a crowded sheet.

Hard requirements for that prompt:

- Its **first sentence** states this is a **single square sheet of separate cutout pieces in flat vector style on a plain flat white background**. Opening words set the mode, and rendering an actual decorated cake is the failure it is most prone to.
- It lists the pieces explicitly as a numbered list.
- It states the **grid layout with even spacing, nothing overlapping or touching, nothing cropped at the edge**.
- It calls for a **bold closed contour and white keyline border on every piece**, with thick necks, no isolated islands, and a solid base area on each.
- Any lettering appears **in quotes with exact casing**, enclosed in its own backing shape.
- The exclusion list comes **last**, after every positive descriptor, and leads with no cake, no dessert table, no scene, no sticks drawn in, no overlapping pieces.
- It is self-contained: someone pasting it cold, with no memory of this conversation, gets the same image. No "as described above", no "the pieces from earlier".
- One prompt only. No variants, no alternates, no commentary after the code block.
